RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made between Sample Property Group LLC ("Landlord") and the Tenant named below, for the unit at 100 Example Street, Unit 4B, Sampletown.

1. Term. The lease term begins on November 1, 2026 and ends on October 31, 2027.

2. Rent. Monthly rent is $1,150, due on the 1st of each month.

3. Late fees. A late fee of $150 is charged if rent is not received by the 3rd of the month, plus $25 for each additional day.

4. Security deposit. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,300, equal to two months' rent, before receiving keys.

5. Non-refundable portion. $500 of the security deposit is non-refundable and will be kept as a cleaning fee regardless of the condition of the unit.

6. Move-in condition. Tenant must submit a written list of existing damage within 48 hours of move-in. Any damage not listed within this period is deemed to have been caused by Tenant.

7. Deposit return. The deposit, less deductions, will be returned within 60 days after Tenant vacates and provides a forwarding address.

8. Landlord entry. Landlord may enter the unit at any time without prior notice for inspections, repairs, or showings.

9. Early termination. If Tenant ends the lease early, Tenant shall pay an early termination fee equal to three months' rent and remains responsible for rent until the unit is re-let.

10. Utilities and fees. Tenant is responsible for electricity, water, internet, and a flat $40 monthly trash and amenity fee.

11. Repairs. Tenant shall pay for repair of any appliance failure costing under $300.

12. Guests. Overnight guests staying more than 5 nights in a month require written consent from Landlord and a $20 per night fee.

13. Renewal. This lease renews automatically for twelve months unless Tenant gives written notice 90 days before the end date.
